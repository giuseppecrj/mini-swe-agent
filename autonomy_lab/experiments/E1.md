## Design

As in LOOP_SPEC §2.3:

- each task gets 16 trials;
- every decision receives each channel (`none`, `contract`, `check`, `both`) 4 times, 2 with filler and 2 without;
- every decision has P4 exposure on both sides;
- execution order is shuffled with the recorded seed.

The full design is written to `design.json` before the first trial.

## Predictions and decision rules

These are the components, regions and verdict rules of LOOP_SPEC §3.2, including the pre-registration clarifications, applied unchanged:

- **P1 (H1):** a sign-test component over all planned tasks, with ties and excluded tasks counting against the tested direction; pooled settled rate ≥ 0.90; pooled unsettled rate ≤ 0.50. Arbitrary decisions only.
- **P2 (H2):** |contract-only − check-only| < 10 pp, in both the arbitrary and the inferable stratum.
- **P3, P4:** *[freeze: per the G2 decision; recommended descriptive only, with estimates and CIs reported and no verdict]*.
- **Validity:** any failed gate in LOOP_SPEC §2.4 makes every verdict `invalid`. Unreviewed confinement signals make the run `pending_review`.

## Stop rules (LOOP_SPEC §8.3)

Stop on any of:

- budget cap reached;
- a validity gate fails twice after a fix attempt;
- a provider usage limit or model deprecation;
- a Pi version change;
- any confirmed confinement violation or spec tampering.

An interrupted run is reported as such, never analysed as complete.

## Reporting

Every verdict goes into REPORT.md, supported, falsified, inconclusive or invalid alike. Each is reported with:

- the run ID;
- this file's commit;
- the bank hash;
- the analysis commit.

Derived per-decision data containing no model text goes under `autonomy_lab/results/<run-id>/` (D5).
